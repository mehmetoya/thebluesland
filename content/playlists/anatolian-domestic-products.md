If "Masterpieces of Erkin the Father" is a single artist's case for Anadolu rock, this is the whole movement's: five hundred tracks spanning the genre's 1960s-70s golden age and the artists it influenced since. Barış Manço and Cem Karaca sit alongside dozens of deeper cuts, building a fuller picture of what happened when Anatolian folk melody met fuzz-guitar and psychedelia.

The deepest Anadolu rock listen in this catalogue — treat it as a long-term project, not a single sitting.
