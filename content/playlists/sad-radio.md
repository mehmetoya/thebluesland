A companion piece to the deepest blues archives in this catalogue — Bessie Smith's aching vocal power, Blind Willie Johnson's gospel-blues, the first generation of Delta guitarists captured on record. This is the blues before electricity, before the backing band, when a voice and a guitar were the whole arrangement.
