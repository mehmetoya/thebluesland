Some playlists are for driving, some for parties; this one is for the evenings that need talking down. It's built on the steadier, more soulful side of the blues — Al Green's warmth as much as Albert King's sting — on the theory that the genre earned its name by being honest about hard nights, and that honesty is its own kind of relief.

A slow-evening record, in the most literal sense.
