A large, unhurried folk and singer-songwriter collection built for exactly the setting the name suggests — quiet company rather than a centerpiece. Aldous Harding's idiosyncratic voice and Aoife O'Donovan's bluegrass-adjacent songwriting sit among two hundred and eighty tracks of similarly gentle acoustic music.

Made to run in the background of a slow conversation.
