The title is a dare: if you think you know the blues, this is the list that tests it. It leans toward the rawer, guitar-forward end of the genre — players who treat the twelve-bar form as a starting point rather than a cage — and mixes decades freely, so a modern player like Ash Grunwald sits comfortably next to older hands.

Best late at night, loud enough that the guitar tone actually matters.
