A focused tribute rather than a genre mix: Chris Cornell's full range across Soundgarden's grunge-era intensity, Temple of the Dog's one-off elegy for Andrew Wood, and Audioslave's later, more radio-built sound. Few rock vocalists had his range or his control of it, and this collects the evidence across every band he fronted.

A eulogy in playlist form.
