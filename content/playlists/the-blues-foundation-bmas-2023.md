The Blues Foundation's annual awards are the genre's closest thing to an industry census — a yearly reminder that blues isn't a museum piece but a living scene with new records and new players every year. This collects the 2023 nominees and honorees, current blues alongside veterans like Buddy Guy still setting the bar.

A good answer to "what's happening in blues right now."
