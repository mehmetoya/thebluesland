"Slowhand" is Clapton's own nickname, and this playlist is built entirely around what earned it: the guitar solos, across every band he's been part of, from Cream's power-trio explorations to Derek and the Dominos' more anguished electric blues. It's a career retrospective told through six strings rather than album chronology.

For anyone who wants to hear one guitarist's whole range in under an hour.
