The title borrows an old blues maxim — that rock and roll, gospel, and just about everything else in American popular music owes the blues a debt it can never repay. This collection leans into that lineage: rawer, older, more Delta and Chicago than polished, built around the idea that the genre's power was always in its honesty rather than its production.

A deep-catalogue listen, best treated as a long-running background rather than a curated set.
