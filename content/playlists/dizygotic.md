"Dizygotic" describes fraternal twins — related, but not identical — and that's the organizing idea here: David Bowie's glam rock, the Bee Gees' falsetto pop, Donny Hathaway's soul and Cream's blues-rock all treated as siblings rather than strangers. It's a playlist built on unexpected pairings holding together anyway.

Good for a listener who likes being surprised by what comes next.
