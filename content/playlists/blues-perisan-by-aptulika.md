A personal, slightly chaotic crate-dig: European psychedelic blues-rock (Amon Düül II, Asia Minor) shares space with Barış Manço and the Turkish psychedelic scene that was listening to the same records and answering back in its own language. "Perişan" is doing some ironic work in the title — this is a mess in the best sense, held together by fuzz-guitar tone more than genre purity.

One of the more unpredictable playlists in the catalogue; good for a late drive when you want to be surprised.
