A concentration playlist in the literal sense — solo piano and minimalist composition from Dustin O'Halloran and Chad Lawson, chosen specifically for what it doesn't do: demand attention. A hundred tracks of the same restrained register, built to sit underneath work rather than compete with it.

Functional music, and unapologetically so.
