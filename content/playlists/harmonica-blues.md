Every blues instrument has its own lineage, and this playlist follows the harmonica's: the Chicago players who turned a pocket-sized instrument into a lead voice as expressive as any guitar. Big Walter Horton and Charlie Musselwhite are the anchor points, but the whole collection is a study in tone and phrasing on one instrument.

Headphones recommended — the detail in the playing is the whole point.
