This week we said goodbye to a founding guitarist, the charts welcomed a major platform back, and Oasis kept things true to form. Five stories, five minutes.

## Richard Lloyd of Television has died

Richard Lloyd, who co-founded Television alongside Tom Verlaine, Billy Ficca, and Richard Hell, has died at 75. Geese paid tribute to the late guitarist by covering the band's track "Ain't That Nothin'".

*Why it matters:* Television was a pioneering rock group whose enduring influence is still heard in bands like Geese today.

## YouTube returns to the Billboard charts

A year after pulling its numbers from chart consideration, YouTube has reversed course and brought its data back. The platform confirmed its return on Monday following a change in how Billboard weighs free and paid streaming.

*Why it matters:* Charts steer labels and listeners alike, and which plays count directly determines who claims the number one spot.

Sources: [Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/billboard-changes-free-vs-paid-streaming-formula-on-charts-and-youtube-is-bringing-its-data-back/) · [Stereogum](https://stereogum.com/2517130/youtube-streams-will-once-again-count-toward-billboard-charts/news/) · [Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/story/youtube-squashes-feud-with-billboard-charts/)

## Oasis will skip their Rock Hall induction

Despite finally entering the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame, Noel and Liam Gallagher will not attend the Los Angeles ceremony. The brothers will skip the event entirely rather than show up to accept the honor or perform.

*Why it matters:* For most groups the Rock Hall is a career pinnacle, but two empty seats turn this induction into a statement about attitude over awards.

Sources: [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/oasis-wont-be-attending-rock-and-roll-fame-induction-1235654642/) · [Consequence](https://consequence.net/2026/11/oasis-skip-rock-and-roll-hall-of-fame-induction-ceremony/) · [Stereogum](https://stereogum.com/2517176/oasis-wont-attend-their-rock-roll-hall-of-fame-induction/news/)

## GTA VI is throwing a music festival

A one-day festival is coming to Miami to mark the release of Grand Theft Auto. The lineup features Travis Scott and Cardi B alongside DJ sets from Justice, The Dare, and Fcukers.

*Why it matters:* A video game launch now claims a coveted spot on the festival calendar, proving games are building actual stages for music.

Sources: [Consequence](https://consequence.net/2026/11/grand-theft-auto-vi-the-festival-travis-scott-cardi-b-and-justice/) · [Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/story/gta-vi-is-putting-on-a-music-festival/) · [NME](https://www.nme.com/news/music/grand-theft-auto-6-the-festival-announced-with-travis-scott-cardi-b-justice-more-3975480)

## The Strokes hit SNL and unveil puppets

The Strokes performed "Lonely in the Future" and "Going Shopping" on SNL without Nick Valensi. The band also shared a new music video for "Lonely in the Future" featuring Spitting Image puppets that take aim at Elon Musk and the billionaire class.

*Why it matters:* The band managed to launch a sharp critique across both live television and a puppet-filled video to reach well beyond their usual audience.
